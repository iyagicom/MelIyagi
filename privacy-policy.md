# Privacy Policy for MelIyagi

**Last updated: October 21, 2026**

## Overview
MelIyagi is an anonymous downloader for Freenet (Hyphanet), I2P and BitTorrent.

## Data Collection
MelIyagi does **not** collect, store, or transmit any personal data to the developer. Nothing is stored by the developer. It has no accounts, no analytics, and no advertising.

## Network Use
The app connects to the networks you choose (Freenet, I2P, BitTorrent, optionally through Tor) to download the links you provide.
